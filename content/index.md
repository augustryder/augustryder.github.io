Movement alone can succeed where deliberation fails.... Only activity, movement is productive. Then why not start moving right away? But comfort!? Comfort avoids movement; it therefore does not take up the search.... Only action, movement, produces what could truly be called education or culture (_Bildung_).

— Arnold Schoenberg, _Theory of Harmony_ (1911)
